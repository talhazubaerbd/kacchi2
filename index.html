Why this dish

One pot that holds a whole culture

Ethnobotany studies how people use plants. Kacchi biryani is a perfect case: every ingredient is a chosen plant part, every step is knowledge handed from cook to cook, and the finished pot belongs to weddings, Eid and family.

How plants become a tradition

Families share

The meal marks weddings and Eid and carries the recipe forward.
